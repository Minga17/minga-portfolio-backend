WHO
01 Introduction

Who I Am

Some people find their path early. I found mine by moving across continents and learning to adapt along the way. Born in the DRC, raised in Zimbabwe, now building toward something bigger in the USA, my story isn't just about geography. It's about becoming.

I'm a student with a quiet kind of drive. Not the loudest person in the room, but consistently the most focused. I move through life blending two worlds that most people think don't go together: the creative energy of music and soccer, and the logical precision of technology. That tension? It's actually my biggest strength.

Right now, I'm focused on data analysis, finding patterns in messy datasets, understanding what the numbers are actually saying, and turning raw information into something that makes decisions easier. So far I've analysed over 21,000 rows of real-world data across two completed projects, with more in progress.

I'm here to build skills, build a career in data, and build something worth being proud of.

ROOTS
02 Personal Background

Where I'm From

Born in the Democratic Republic of Congo. Raised in Zimbabwe. Currently writing the next chapter in the United States.

That's not just a travel itinerary, that's a curriculum. Growing up in Zimbabwe shaped something in me that no classroom could: the ability to stay grounded when things get uncertain, to find focus when resources are limited, and to keep moving forward even when the path isn't clear. It also taught me to look at what the numbers actually say, not what I wish they said. That instinct is what makes a good analyst.

Every environment I've lived in has left a mark. The DRC gave me origin. Zimbabwe gave me roots and resilience. America is giving me a canvas. I carry all three wherever I go.

"You don't need a perfect start to build something meaningful. You just need a reason to keep going."
03 Interests and Passions

What Drives Me

I live at the intersection of movement and stillness. On the field, I'm competitive soccer teaches you things no lecture ever can: reading the situation in real time, communicating without words, pushing through when your legs don't cooperate. That discipline bleeds into everything else I do.

Off the field, I pick up my guitar. Music is where I process the world, where logic meets emotion, where I let myself be unstructured. There's something powerful about creating something out of nothing, and that same instinct pulls me toward data and problem-solving.

Data is where those two instincts converge. Every dataset has a pattern waiting to be found, and finding it feels like both a logical puzzle and a creative act.

05 Skills and Strengths

What I Bring

Some skills come from classrooms. Mine come from data, discipline, and three different countries.

Technical · Data Tools

🐍

Python

Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib. I use Python to load messy data, clean it, explore it, and build visualizations that actually communicate something real.

🗄️

SQL

Writing queries to pull, filter, and join data across tables. If the answer lives in a database, SQL is how I find it.

📊

Data Visualization

Matplotlib, Seaborn, and learning Tableau. A good chart is the difference between data that's seen and data that gets ignored.

🧹

Data Cleaning

Real data is never clean. Nulls, duplicates, wrong formats, mislabeled columns, this is where most of the actual work lives, and I'm not afraid of it.

📁

Excel / Spreadsheets

PivotTables, VLOOKUP, conditional logic. Still one of the most-used data tools in any workplace and I know my way around it.

🔢

Statistics Fundamentals

Mean, median, distributions, correlation. Knowing what a number actually means before drawing any conclusions from it.

Foundation · Transferable

🧠

Analytical Thinking

I break problems down before jumping to answers. From reading a soccer match in real time to debugging a dataset, pattern recognition is how I naturally operate.

🤝

Communication

Data only matters if you can explain it. I've learned to translate complex ideas into clear language, from three languages, three countries, and a lot of practice.

🎯

Consistency

I show up. Not because I always feel like it because discipline builds what motivation alone never could. Every day is a data point in a longer trend.

🌍

Adaptability

Three countries. Multiple environments. Different challenges. I've learned to figure it out, wherever "it" is. New tools, new datasets, new problems included.
