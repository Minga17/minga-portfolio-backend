Interests and Passions

What Drives Me

Outside of data and code, you will find me doing one of four things. Playing soccer, gaming, watching cartoons, or making music.

I am serious about all of them. I can wake up in the middle of the night and just start watching soccer highlights or jump into a game without a second thought. Not because I planned to. Just because that is who I am. I do not need a reason to love the things I love.

Soccer has been part of my life since Zimbabwe. It is where I learned to compete, to read situations fast, and to trust the people around me. Those same instincts show up when I am working through a dataset. You learn to see patterns quickly. You learn to make decisions without having all the information.

Music is something I am building toward in my own time. I have a home studio that I am still setting up, which means there is another project always waiting for me outside of data. Making music and analysing data feel more similar than people think. Both start with something raw and messy. Both end with something that tells a story.

Gaming taught me persistence. You do not give up when the data does not make sense on the first try. You figure out what you are missing and you try again.

And cartoons? That one I cannot fully explain. But I stand by it completely.
